Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This page explains the current privacy practices of LovePDFi Photo Studio at images.lovepdfi.com.

Images you select

The current editor opens selected JPG, PNG or WEBP files in your browser and renders edits using the browser Canvas API. The editor does not include an image-upload endpoint and does not transmit the selected image to a LovePDFi image-processing server. Your image remains in the active browser session unless you choose to download or otherwise share it yourself.

Local processing and temporary session

Editing is performed on your device. The current version does not provide cloud saving or user accounts. Refreshing or closing the tab clears the active editing session. Download your edited image before leaving the page.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This release does not include Google Analytics, advertising tags or third-party tracking scripts, and it does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting and content-delivery provider may process standard connection and security information (such as IP address, browser details and request time) to deliver and protect the website. If analytics, advertising, consent tools or other third-party services are added later, this policy must be updated to identify them, explain the data they collect and provide any consent choices required by applicable law and platform policies.

Third-party resources

The current production files are designed to use local CSS and JavaScript assets and do not require an external photo-processing API. When you follow a link to another website, that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

This tool is a general-purpose photo editor and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Do not upload or share images unless you have the right to use them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the website's features or data practices change. The revision date above should be updated whenever the policy is materially changed.

Contact

For questions or support, email support@lovepdfi.com or visit the main LovePDFi website.
