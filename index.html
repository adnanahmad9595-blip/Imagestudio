LOVEpdfi PHOTO STUDIO

Create better photos with online effects, frames and shape crops

LovePDFi Photo Studio is a browser-based photo editor for adding visual effects, decorative frames, creative filters and shaped photo crops. Choose an image from your device, select a style, adjust the settings and export your edited result. The editor is designed for quick creative projects such as profile pictures, invitations, greeting images, social posts and personal photo designs.

Photo effects and overlays

Explore five effect collections: Nature, Luxury, Light, Weather and Artistic. The library includes styles such as cherry blossom, rose petals, butterflies, autumn leaves, snowfall, bokeh, lens flare, glass-inspired details, neon glow, golden dust, smoke, fire, prism, confetti, vintage grain, halftone and glitch-inspired overlays. Select an effect and use the transparency slider to control how strongly it appears. Compatible overlays also let you adjust color and scale.

Decorative photo frames

Choose from Classic, Floral, Celebration and Creative frame groups. Available styles include golden, silver, minimal, black, wooden, Polaroid, film strip, floral, rose, birthday, wedding, love, baby, family, Christmas, Eid, Ramadan, vintage, neon, magazine and nature-inspired borders. Change frame color, thickness and transparency to suit your photo.

Crop a photo into a shape

Use circle, heart, star, hexagon, oval, diamond, triangle, rounded rectangle, cloud, flower, arch, shield, speech bubble, leaf, pentagon or cross masks. Shape cropping is useful for profile images, stickers, invitations and creative layouts. Adjust the fill zoom to change how the image sits inside the selected mask.

Creative photo filters

Apply looks such as Vintage, Cinematic, Black & White, Warm, Cool, Dreamy, Vivid, Matte, Fade, Noir, Retro, Pastel, Golden and Dramatic. Fine-tune brightness, contrast, saturation and blur to make the final image fit your design. The Original option returns the filter look to its neutral setting.

How to use the online photo editor

  1. Upload a photo. Choose a JPG, PNG or WEBP image from your device, or drag it into the upload area. The current editor accepts images up to 25 MB.
  2. Choose a creative tool. Open Effects, Filters, Frames or Shapes and select a style from its category.
  3. Customize the result. Adjust transparency, effect color, frame thickness, zoom, brightness, contrast, saturation or blur where those controls are available.
  4. Export your image. Select PNG or JPG, set JPG quality and download the edited image to your device.

Designed for private, local photo editing

The editor uses the browser's Canvas features to render the selected image and apply effects. In this version, the photo file is not uploaded to a LovePDFi image-processing server. Your browser needs enough available memory to work with large images, and closing or refreshing the tab clears the current editing session. Download your result before leaving the page.

Supported formats and practical tips

  • Input formats: JPG/JPEG, PNG and WEBP.
  • Export formats: PNG for lossless output with transparency support where present in the canvas, or JPG for a smaller file.
  • Image size: Images up to 25 MB can be selected in this version; performance depends on your device and browser memory.
  • Best results: Start with a clear, well-lit image. Use subtle overlays when you want the original subject to remain prominent.
  • Color control: The color picker affects compatible decorative overlays and frame borders; some effects retain their natural multi-color appearance.

Frequently asked questions

Is LovePDFi Photo Studio free to use?

Yes. The tools in this version are available without an account or payment. Standard browser and device limitations still apply.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No image-upload service is used by this editor. The selected photo is opened and processed in your browser. If you add third-party analytics or advertising to the website in the future, update the privacy notice to explain those services separately.

Which image formats can I edit?

You can open JPG/JPEG, PNG and WEBP images. You can export the finished image as PNG or JPG.

Can I change an effect's transparency and color?

Yes. Use the transparency slider to make a selected overlay lighter or stronger. Compatible effects also support a color picker and an effect-size control. Some artistic effects use their own colors.

Can I crop a photo into a heart or circle?

Yes. Select Shapes and choose Circle, Heart or another available mask. Use Shape Fill Zoom to adjust the image inside the shape.

Why can a large image feel slow?

Photo editing takes place in your device's memory. Large dimensions, limited RAM or an older browser can slow rendering. Try a smaller copy of the image and close unused tabs if performance is poor.

Will the editor keep my work if I close the tab?

No. This version does not provide cloud projects or persistent editing sessions. Download your result before refreshing or closing the browser tab.
