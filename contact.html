Contact & Support

LovePDFi Photo Studio is part of the LovePDFi family of online tools. For questions, bug reports or tool requests, email support@lovepdfi.com. You can also visit the main LovePDFi website.

Before contacting support

  • Include the page URL where the issue occurred.
  • Describe the browser and device you are using.
  • Explain the steps that caused the problem and any error message shown.
  • Do not send private photos or sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you are comfortable sharing it.

What to include in a support request

Please include the tool name, your device and browser, and a short description of what went wrong. Do not email personal photos or sensitive files unless sharing them is necessary and you have considered the privacy implications. This static website does not collect support requests through an on-page form.
